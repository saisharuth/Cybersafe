🎣 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where scammers pretend to be trusted people or companies to steal personal information.

🚨 Warning Signs

1. Suspicious links

2. Messages asking for OTP

3. Urgent account-block warnings

4. Unknown senders

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

1. Never share your OTP.

2. Avoid suspicious links.

3. Verify the sender.

4. Use official websites and apps.

📱 OTP & Scam Safety

Learn to recognise common online scams and protect yourself!

🔑 OTP Safety

Never share your OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank.

📞 Fake Calls

Scammers may pretend to be bank officers or police. Don't trust callers just because they sound official.

🎁 Prize Scams

If a message says you won a prize but must pay a fee first, be suspicious.

🔗 Suspicious Links

Don't click unexpected links asking for passwords, OTPs or banking details.

💸 UPI Payment Scams

You don't need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Never approve unknown payment requests.
